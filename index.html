My Favorite Highlights of Costa Rica

  1. My top highlight of Costa Rica is the beautiful beaches. The beaches are very clean and the water is very clear. I would love to go there and relax on the beach.
  2. My next favorite feature are the birds. Costa Rica is home to over 15 species of parrots, parakeets, and macaws, including the iconic Scarlet Macaw, Great Green Macaw, and Crimson-fronted Parakeet. These birds thrive in rainforests, coastal forests, and even urban areas with fruiting trees.
  3. The final highlight I want to mention is the adorable wildlife. Costa Rica is home to a diverse range of animals, from sloths and monkeys to sea turtles and toucans. Encountering these creatures in their natural habitat would be an incredible experience. I especially would love to see a baby sloth in real life
